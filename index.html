Customer Segments

So who are these customers?

The model grouped 8,950 credit card customers into three distinct behavioral personas.

Active Transactors

Cluster 0 · 1,275 customers (14.2%)

These customers spend regularly, use their credit limits, and tend to pay consistently.

WHO THEY ARE

High purchase frequency, low cash advance reliance, steady balance payoff.

WHY WE CARE

Retention and loyalty.

WHAT WE COULD DO

Premium rewards and personalized offers.

Cash Advance Revolvers

Cluster 1 · 1,561 customers (17.4%)

These customers rely heavily on cash advances and tend to carry larger balances.

WHO THEY ARE

Frequent cash withdrawals, elevated balance ratios, higher repayment risk.

WHY WE CARE

Revenue vs. risk balance.

WHAT WE COULD DO

Risk-aware offers and balance-management strategies.

Inactive / Low-Activity Users

Cluster 2 · 6,114 customers (68.3%)

These customers have credit available but barely use it.

WHO THEY ARE

Low transaction volume, minimal balance, substantial dormant credit lines.

WHY WE CARE

Reactivation potential.

WHAT WE COULD DO

Targeted re-engagement campaigns.

Technical Methodology

What's happening under the hood?

The model groups customers with similar behavioral patterns without requiring predefined customer labels.

Raw customer data → Cleaning → Feature scaling → K-Means → PCA → Customer segments → Business insights
Python Pandas NumPy Scikit-learn K-Means PCA Streamlit

Analytical Principles

  • Unsupervised Discovery: K-Means identifies behavioral clusters without human bias.
  • Feature Transformation: Log scaling & MinMax normalization handle skewed credit/purchase distributions.
  • Dimensional Validation: PCA maps 17 attributes to 2D component scatter plots.
